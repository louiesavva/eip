Conversations

The Existential Files, 2017

In 2017 Matthew Smith and I, two psychologists and long-time friends, recorded more than sixty conversations about life, the universe and everything. Some were just the two of us. Most had a guest: philosophers, psychologists, sceptics, writers and scientists, among them Susan Blackmore, Chris French and Taner Edis. Sometimes funny, sometimes serious, sometimes profound even.

The book The Existential Files grew out of these conversations. The recordings themselves are all on YouTube.

Searches YouTube’s automatic captions for all 62 episodes, so expect the odd mishearing. Each result links to that moment in the video.
