The Existential Files: chapter guide

Where the book comes from

Matthew Smith and I turned more than sixty conversations into a book, The Existential Files (PDF | EPUB). This guide goes through it chapter by chapter and points to the moments in the recordings each one draws on: 93 of them, from 21 episodes. The times open the video at that point; the episode numbers lead to the Conversations page, where you can also search everything that was said.
